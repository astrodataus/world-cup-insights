Scenario

You scout for a national side

You are an analyst on a national team's staff, preparing for the next tournament. Three World Cups of shots, finishing and creation sit in front of you as one set of joined tables: every shot with its position and expected-goal value, every player's output against what their chances were worth. The question is always the same. Who is producing, who has been riding luck, and where do goals actually come from?

The data

Public match data for the 2018, 2022 and 2026 men's World Cups, parsed into five tables: shots (with pitch position, minute and xG), matches, players, teams and per-player tournament stats. Nothing here is invented; every figure on screen is recomputed from the raw files by an automated check before it ships.

The views

The Tournament is the standings of shooting: how many shots, goals and expected goals each tournament produced, and where the danger comes from. The Pitch draws every shot where it was taken; click one and it explains itself. Finishing puts goals against expected goals, so over- and under-performers separate. The Race replays a match as cumulative xG, minute by minute. Creators ranks the players who make chances rather than take them.

A demo, not a scouting department. But if your business has events with a value attached, this is the same product.
